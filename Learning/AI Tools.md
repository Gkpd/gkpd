## Google Gemini

Source: LinkedIn Learning<br>
Course: Productivity with Google Gemini

### Notable takeaways:

*Prompt Crafting*<br>
- Clear and Concise: Ensure your prompts are straightforward and easy to understand.<br>
- Use a Persona: Assign a role to Gemini for more tailored responses (e.g., "Act as a time management coach").<br>
- Provide Context: Give background information and details surrounding your request.<br>
- Define Tasks: Specify the tasks or steps Gemini needs to follow.<br>
- Refine and Guide: Continuously correct and guide Gemini based on its responses.<br>

*Use the B.R.A.I.N framework:*<br>
Background - Set the scene and provide context<br>
Role Assignment - Define Gemini's role, e.g "act as..."<br>
Action - Specify the task and format<br>
Inputs - Include necessary details and requirements<br>
Narrowing Down - Refine the request and set boundaries<br>
<br>
example:<br>
Background = Organise my day for maximum focus and delivery<br>
Role Asignment = Act as a time management coach<br>
Action = Break my day into time blocks for work, exercise and relaxtion<br>
Input = emails, planning and a workout must be included<br>
Narrowing down = Prioritise deep work for the morning, but only for 60 minutes<br>
